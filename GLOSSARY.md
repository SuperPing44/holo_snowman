# Holo Snowman

A blank physical snowman that visitors decorate by drawing and placing stickers on a tablet; their decoration is projected live onto the statue.

## The physical snowman

**Statue**:
The physical, blank snowman that is projected onto, made of three Balls and three Panels.
_Avoid_: Model, sculpture, figure

**Ball**:
One of the three 3D spheres of the Statue: **Head**, **Torso** and **Base** (top to bottom).
_Avoid_: Sphere, circle, snowball

**Hat Panel**:
The flat board behind the Head, used for a hat or hair.
_Avoid_: Plain 1, plane, cardboard

**Arm Panel**:
One of the two flat, angled boards attached to the Torso (**left** and **right**), used for arms.
_Avoid_: Plain 2, plane, stick

**Surface**:
Any part of the Statue that can be projected onto: a Ball or a Panel.
_Avoid_: Area, zone, region

## Decorating

**Visitor**:
The person who decorates the snowman at the event, one at a time.
_Avoid_: User, player, customer

**Stroke**:
One continuous freehand line a Visitor draws.
_Avoid_: Line, path, drawing

**Sticker**:
A ready-made image a Visitor places, moves and resizes on the snowman.
_Avoid_: Stamp, decal, emoji

**Design**:
Everything one Visitor has made: all their Strokes and Stickers together.
_Avoid_: Drawing, artwork, customization
